পাঠ ৫.১ - The Two-Letter Sound Slide (ba, ca, da, fa, ma, pa, ra, sa, ta)
Continuous phonation methodology

The Two-Letter Sound Slide teaches children to blend two sounds smoothly: ba, ca, da, fa, ma, pa, ra, sa, ta Keep voice ON between sounds - no stopping or restarting. This develops smooth blending skills essential for reading fluency. Spoken: "I see a..." (আমি একটা দেখি...) This encourages children to complete the sentence with the blended sound.

Spoken Routine

I see a... (আমি একটা দেখি...) I see a bat! (আমি একটা বæt দেখি!) I see a cat! (আমি একটা বিল্লি দেখি!) I see a dad! (আমি একটা داد দেখি!) Great blending! (শानदार मिश्रण!)

Home Activity

Sound slide practice: Use a slide or ramp at the playground or make one at home with cardboard. Place letter pairs at top: ba, ca, da, etc. Child slides down saying the blended sound. Make it a daily fun activity!

পাঠ ৫.২ - 3-Sound Arm Tapping & Blending (s-a-t -> sat, m-a-t -> mat)
3-Sound Arm Tapping & Blending

3-Sound Arm Tapping & Blending technique: Tap left shoulder for first sound, right shoulder for second sound, chest for third sound. Example: s-a-t -> sat Tap left shoulder: /s/ Tap right shoulder: /a/ Tap chest: /t/ Say smoothly: sat Another example: m-a-t -> mat This develops multisensory blending skills.

Spoken Routine

Sit on the mat. (मत पर बैठो!) Can you hear the s-a-t blend? (स-ए-ट मिश्रण सुनते हो?) Listen for the sat sound! (साट सुनो!) Great blending! (शानदार मिश्रण!)
