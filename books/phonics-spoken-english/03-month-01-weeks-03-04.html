পাঠ ৩.১ - /c/ এবং /k/ শব্দ (কাস্টানেট_action)
Castanets Action

/c/ এবং /k/ উভয়ই একই শব্দ তৈরি করে (ক harsh zástupce s 이음). শিশুকে সिखান যে এই দুটি অক্ষরের ljud je isti. Castanets bajakar taali bajayen - "kak kak kak" शabad sunen. Spoken: "Look at the cat!" (बिल्ली देखो!) This helps children understand that different letters can make the same sound.

Spoken Routine

Look at the cat! (बिल्ली देखो!) Can you hear the /k/ sound? (क sound सुनते हो?) Listen for the castanets! (कास्टानेट्स सुनो!) Great listening! (शानदार सुनवाई!)

Home Activity

Sound matching game: Show pictures of cat, kite, cup, king. Ask child to group /k/ sound words together. Practice 5 minutes daily.

পাঠ ৩.২ - /e/ শব্দ (ডিম ফো Rose action)
Short /e/ (এ) Cracking Egg Action

Make the short /e/ sound as in "egg", "bed", "red": Mouth slightly open, lips relaxed, tongue mid-level. Crucial contrast: /æ/ (অ্যা as in pan) vs. /e/ (এ as in pen). /æ/ is wider mouth opening like saying "ah", /e/ is more closed mouth like saying "eh". This distinction is essential for proper phonics development. Practice by saying "pan" vs "pen", "bat" vs "bet".

Spoken Routine

Eat your egg. (अंडा खाओ!) Can you hear the /e/ sound? (ए sound सुनते हो?) Listen for the cracking egg! (अंडा फूटने की आवाज सुनो!) Excellent listening! (शानदार सुनवाई!)

Home Activity

Egg sound detection: Listen for /e/ sound in: Cracking eggs, Door creaking, Fan switching on, Water dripping. Make it a daily listening game!

Panting Breath /h/ and Playful Puppy /r/ with rag

Yummy Tummy /m/ (closed lips hum) & Banging Drum /d/ (tongue tap)

Make /m/ sound like yummy tummy: Lips closed, humming like enjoying food. Voiced bilabial nasal (vocal cords vibrate, air through nose). This develops nasal vs stop consonant discrimination.

Spoken Routine
